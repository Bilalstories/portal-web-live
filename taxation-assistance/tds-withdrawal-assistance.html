📋 Service Details

TDS ka paisa wapas nikalne (refund lene) ka ekmatra tarika Income Tax Return (ITR) file karna hai. Agar aapka tax banta nahi hai ya kam banta hai aur TDS jyada kat gaya hai, to aap use niche diye gaye tarike se wapas le sakte hain:Check Tax Credit: Sabse pehle aapko Public Portal par login karke apna Form 26AS aur AIS (Annual Information Statement) check karna hoga. Isme aapke kate hue TDS ki saari details dikhai deti hain.Bank Account Validation: Paisa wapas pane ke liye aapka bank account Public Portal par Pre-validated hona mandatory hai, jisse refund ka paisa sidhe aapke bank account me aa sake.File ITR: Har saal sahi samay par apni income ke mutabik (jaise ITR-1 ya ITR-2) Public Portal par e-file karein. Apne saare investments aur deductions (jaise 80C, 80D) wahan darj karein.E-Verification: ITR form submit karne ke baad use Aadhaar OTP ke jariye verify karein. Successful verification ke baad, processed tax refund ka paisa kuch hi hafton me aapke bank account me credit kar diya jata hai.
